# Squad Composition Rules

## Squad Size

A franchise must have a **minimum of 18 players** and a **maximum of 25 players** in its squad.
A squad that ends the auction below 18 players is not legal and must be topped up.

## Overseas Player Limit

A squad may contain at most **8 overseas players**. In the playing eleven, at most **4 overseas
players** may take the field. This is the single most common squad-building constraint: signing a
ninth overseas player is not permitted, and signing a seventh or eighth overseas player who cannot
break into the best four is dead money.

Because only four overseas players can play, the practical rule of thumb is to buy overseas players
only for roles where they are clearly better than the best available Indian option, and to buy one
or two overseas players as cover for the first-choice four.

## Indian Core

With at least seven of the eleven being Indian, the Indian core carries the squad. A balanced Indian
core normally needs: two opening batters, two or three middle-order batters, a wicketkeeper, an
Indian all-rounder who bowls, and three or four Indian bowlers across pace and spin.

## Uncapped Players

Uncapped Indian players are cheap and count fully towards squad size. They are the standard way to
fill the back half of a 25-man squad without spending purse, leaving money for the first-choice XI.

## Impact Player Rule

Each team may name additional substitutes and use one **Impact Player** per match, who can be
introduced at a designated point and replace a player in the eleven. The Impact Player must be
Indian unless the team has fewer than four overseas players in its starting eleven.

The practical effect is that a team can bat deeper or bowl an extra specialist than the eleven
alone allows, so squads now value a specialist finisher or a specialist death bowler who would not
previously have fitted into a balanced eleven.
