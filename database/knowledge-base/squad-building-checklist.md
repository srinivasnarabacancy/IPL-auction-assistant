# Squad Building Checklist

Before an auction, a team should know exactly which slots it is shopping for. A complete IPL squad
covers the following, and the assistant checks a user's squad against this list.

## Minimum Viable Spine

1. Two opening batters, at least one Indian.
2. Two middle-order batters who score well against spin.
3. A wicketkeeper who bats in the top six, plus a backup keeper.
4. A finisher who can strike at 175 or better in the last four overs.
5. A frontline all-rounder who genuinely bowls four overs.
6. Two new-ball bowlers, at least one Indian.
7. A death-overs specialist.
8. Two frontline spinners, ideally one wrist-spinner and one finger-spinner.
9. Backup for every overseas first-choice player, because availability is never guaranteed.

## Balance Checks

- **Role distribution.** A 25-man squad is usually around 8 batters, 6 all-rounders, 7 bowlers and
  2 to 3 wicketkeepers. Heavy skew in any direction means the eleven cannot be balanced.
- **Overseas count.** At most 8, and the best 4 must cover 4 different roles.
- **Left-right balance** in the batting order and **pace-spin balance** in the attack.
- **Age profile.** A squad entirely of players over 33 will decline together.

## Reading the Numbers in This Dataset

- Batting **strike rate** matters more than average for middle-order and finishing roles.
- Batting **average** matters more for openers and anchors.
- Bowling **economy** is the primary number for death and middle-overs bowlers.
- Bowling **average** and wickets matter more for powerplay strike bowlers.
- The **rating** field is a single composite 0-100 score combining impact, role fit and recent
  form, and is useful for a quick comparison but should never override the role-specific numbers.
